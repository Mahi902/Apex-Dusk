# Apex Dusk: Open City

> A 3D open-world driving sandbox that runs right in your browser. Cruise the city, outrun the police and take delivery jobs from dusk till dawn.

## About

Apex Dusk is a neon-soaked open city you can explore on four wheels or on foot. There is no account and no store to visit. Open it, hit **Play**, and install it as an app to keep it on your device and play offline.

## Features

- Open city with traffic, a day and night cycle and a minimap
- Wanted level with police chases
- Delivery jobs, first to the cyan beacon, then to the magenta one
- Drift and near-miss style combos
- Steal cars, hop out and explore on foot
- Inventory with pickups: orange crates are scattered along the sidewalks
- Autosave, so your progress is kept between sessions
- Keyboard, touch and on-screen wheel and pedals
- Chase, high and close cameras, plus first person view
- Graphics options from Low to Ultra, shadow, frame rate and traffic settings
- Drop a `.glb` car file onto the game to drive your own model

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Gas, steer and brake (on foot: move) |
| Space | Handbrake (on foot: jump) |
| Shift | Nitro (on foot: sprint) |
| F | Enter, exit or carjack a vehicle |
| C | Change camera |
| I | Open inventory (keys 1 to 5 use items) |
| P | Pause |

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen wheel, the GAS and BRAKE pedals and the NITRO button. An ENTER button appears next to a vehicle. Drag anywhere to look around and tap + on the hotbar to open your bag. Landscape works best.

## Install

Apex Dusk is a Progressive Web App. Use the **Download** button on the home page to install it.

- **Chrome, Edge, Brave, Samsung Internet:** press **Download**, then confirm.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Firefox and others:** use the browser menu and look for **Install** or **Add to Home Screen**.

After the first launch the game works offline.

## Run locally

Service workers need a web server, so don't open the files by double clicking them. From the project folder run:

```
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000`.
